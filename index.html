Our Story

We're a two-person studio just starting out — and that's exactly the point. No account managers, no layers of process, no juggling your project between twenty other clients. When you work with us, you work directly with the people actually building your product.

We started this company because we saw too many small businesses and startups get stuck with outdated websites, clunky software, or nobody who could explain their tech in plain language. We wanted to fix that — with modern tools, honest communication, and real care about your growth.
